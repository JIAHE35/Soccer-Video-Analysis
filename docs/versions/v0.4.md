# V0.4 - Team and Referee Classification

**Status:** Planned, not implemented

**Git reference:** None yet

**Proposed milestone:** Classify tracked people as Team A, Team B, Referee, or
Unknown while preserving V0.3 tracking and ball behavior.

## Motivation

V0.3 can answer "which bounding boxes belong to the same short-term track?" but
cannot answer "which team or role does this person belong to?" All people are
currently displayed as `player #ID`.

V0.4 is intended to add a role-classification layer after field filtering and
tracking, but before drawing annotations:

```text
YOLO person detection
  -> ByteTrack ID
  -> grass-field filtering
  -> upper-body jersey crop
  -> per-frame color classification
  -> per-track temporal voting
  -> Team A / Team B / Referee / Unknown label
```

Ball detection remains independent at confidence `0.18` and does not receive an
ID.

## Proposed implementation

- Add `src/team_classifier.py` for jersey-region extraction, color features,
  match-specific role classification, and temporal voting.
- Extract the central upper-body portion of each person box before any
  annotations are drawn.
- Use HSV to reject likely grass pixels and Lab color distance to compare the
  remaining jersey pixels with match-specific reference colors.
- Keep an `Unknown` result when the crop is too small, contains too few useful
  pixels, or has an ambiguous color distance.
- Accumulate predictions by ByteTrack ID so that one noisy frame does not
  immediately change a person's displayed team.
- Expire old voting history so reused or stale IDs do not keep an unrelated
  role forever.
- Keep goalkeepers or ambiguous kits as `Unknown` in the first implementation
  instead of forcing an incorrect team or referee label.

## Proposed output

| Artifact | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `outputs/v04_team_classification.mp4` | Video with role-specific labels and colors |
| `outputs/v04_detections.csv` | Per-frame boxes, IDs, roles, and confidence values |

Proposed CSV columns:

```text
frame,time,track_id,role,x1,y1,x2,y2,confidence
```

## Proposed labels

```text
Team A #12 0.83
Team B #7 0.76
Referee #18 0.68
Unknown #23 0.61
ball 0.24
```

The YOLO confidence still describes the `person` detection. Team or referee
classification confidence should be stored separately if exposed in the CSV.

## Acceptance criteria

- V0.3 person IDs continue to appear and the ball remains untracked.
- The two outfield teams use stable, visibly different labels and box colors.
- A single noisy frame does not switch an established track to another team.
- Ambiguous crops become `Unknown` rather than an incorrect confident role.
- Referees are separated when their configured kit color is visible enough.
- The annotated video and CSV contain the same frame, ID, bbox, and role data.
- Automated tests cover crop boundaries, representative kit colors, ambiguous
  colors, temporal voting, stale tracks, and unchanged ball behavior.

## Known risks

- Distant players may have too few jersey pixels for reliable color analysis.
- Shadows, compression, white pitch lines, skin, and overlapping players can
  distort color features.
- Referee and goalkeeper kits may look similar.
- ByteTrack ID switches can transfer or reset role history.
- Match-specific color references prioritize reliable results on the current
  video over immediate generalization to every match.

## Out of scope

- Training a custom player/referee/ball detector.
- Identifying individual players by name or jersey number.
- Reliable identity recovery across camera cuts and replays.
- Continuous ball tracking or interpolation.
- Homography, heatmaps, possession, speed, and distance statistics.

Those items remain candidates for later milestones after V0.4 is evaluated.
